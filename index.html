Welcome to Freiheit2.0 - Europe Adventure

Welcome to Freiheit2.0 - Europe Adventure! My name is Jörg and I will teach you how to play this game. Your goal in this game is to explore the 5 most important countries in the European Union and speak to native speakers in their native language. At the end of the game you will be able to speak simple phrases in 5 languages! Let's go and explore Berlin, which is the Capital city of Germany!
